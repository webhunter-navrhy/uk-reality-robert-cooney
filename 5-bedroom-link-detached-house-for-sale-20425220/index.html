Southfield Villa combines beautifully preserved original features, including a stunning cantilever staircase, fireplaces and sash windows, with superb contemporary family living arranged over three floors. Dating from the 1860s, the property is an impressive 5 bedroomed period townhouse, originally built as the show home for the local brickworks. Set on an attractive, tree-lined avenue only a few minutes’ walk from the centre of Taunton, the property includes a secluded south-facing walled garden and off-street parking. No onward chain.

The accommodation comprises:

•Entrance Hall with stunning cantilever staircase

•Drawing Room with original fireplace and French doors to garden

•Sitting Room with original fireplace

•Open plan Kitchen / Dining Room with Falcon Range, Pantry and French doors to Garden Room

•Garden Room with door to garden

•Utility Room with door to garden

•WC

•Master Bedroom with original fireplace, fitted wardrobe and Ensuite Bathroom with separate shower

•4 further double Bedrooms, all with original fireplaces, on 1st and 2nd floors

•Family Shower Room

•Extensive Storage

Southfield Villa is conveniently situated within a few minutes’ walk of the centre of Taunton with easy access to local amenities, three independent schools, Richard Huish and Bridgwater & Taunton Colleges, Musgrove Park Hospital and the railway station.

Taunton, the County town of Somerset, is a bustling, forward-looking town with excellent amenities, a good selection of independent and high street shops, distinctive restaurants, cafés, a wealth of history and sporting facilities including the County Cricket Ground.

Taunton benefits from a main line railway station linking to London Paddington in less than 2 hours and excellent communications for the M5 motorway at junction 25, situated on the eastern side of the town.
